THREE GRACES

Antonio Canova's statue The Three Graces is a Neoclassical sculpture, in marble, of the mythological three daughters of Zeus—identified on some engravings of the statue as, from left to right, Euphrosyne, Aglaea and Thalia—who were said to represent youth/beauty, mirth, and elegance.

EXPLORE EXHIBITION
Neoclassical Masterpiece

The Essence of Harmony

The sculpture is carved from a single slab of white marble. Canova's assistants roughly blocked out the marble, leaving Canova to finish the final carving and shape the stone to highlight the soft flesh of the Graces. This style was a defining trait of Neoclassicism.

The three figures stand closely together, their bodies almost merging in a dance-like posture that evokes classic elegance and ancient mythologies brought into light.
